# Third-party pricing data

`portkey-model-prices.json` is generated from the
[Portkey-AI/models](https://github.com/Portkey-AI/models) model-pricing
database. That project is licensed under the MIT License; its copyright and
permission notice are available in the upstream
[LICENSE](https://github.com/Portkey-AI/models/blob/main/LICENSE).

Prices are normalized from Portkey's cents-per-token representation to the
original currency per one million tokens. They are list-price estimates, not
proof of the amount charged by a subscription, promotional relay, or reseller.
